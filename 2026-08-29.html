#2

NVIDIA IS TURNING AI DEMAND INTO A FINANCIAL FLYWHEEL

Nvidia posted Q2 revenue of $96.22B, topped EPS estimates ($2.22 vs. $2.09 expected), and guided to roughly 70% revenue growth into fiscal 2028. Shares surged 8.7% and dragged the Nasdaq up 1.6% on the day.

Why it matters

Nvidia is now the single biggest swing factor in US equity indices. When it beats big, it doesn't just move its own stock — it resets the market's entire AI-capex thesis for the next two quarters.

TOP STORY

THE FED JUST GOT A NEW PROBLEM

New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh used his first Jackson Hole address, on August 28, to warn that inflation isn't beaten — saying the central bank "has more work to do" and signaling that further rate hikes, not the cuts markets had priced in, are back on the table.

Why it matters

Markets spent the summer betting on a September cut. A hawkish pivot from a brand-new Fed chair — sworn in just three months ago — changes the calculus for stocks, bonds, and the dollar heading into the next FOMC meeting, and tests how much credibility a new chair wants to spend on an inflation fight investors thought was over.

#3

SALESFORCE'S AI BET STARTS PAYING OFF — WITH ANTHROPIC ALONG FOR THE RIDE

Salesforce shares jumped 22.6% — its best single-day move in years — after posting EPS of $5.90 against a $3.27 estimate and revenue of $11.35B. Executives credited accelerating Agentforce adoption, and the company launched a new "Claudeforce" AI plugin built with Anthropic.

Why it matters

This is one of the first big enterprise-software earnings beats that management explicitly attributes to AI agents actually selling and closing work, not just powering demos — a data point the market has been waiting for.
